# RIDESHARE.DB DATASET RECOMMENDATIONS REPORT

### RECOMMENDATION 1: Strengthen Operations in High-Demand Cities
Houston has the highest number of users, riders, and drivers, making it the platform's strongest market.

**Rommended Actions**:
Prioritize marketing and customer retention programs in Houston.
Increase driver incentives during peak hours.
Introduce loyalty rewards for frequent riders.
Monitor supply-demand balance regularly to maintain service quality.

**Expected Benefit** : Higher customer retention and increased ride volume in the platform's most active city.

### RECOMMENDATION 2: Improve Driver Recruitment in Other Cities
Although Houston has strong driver availability, other cities may require additional driver onboarding to maintain balanced service coverage.

**Recommended Actions**:
Launch driver referral programs.
Offer joining bonuses in cities with lower driver counts.
Conduct targeted recruitment campaigns.

**Expected Benefit** : Reduced rider wait times and improved ride availability across all cities.

### RECOMMENDATION 3: Learn from Top-Rated Cities
New York recorded the highest average driver and rider ratings.

**Recommended Actions**:
Study operational practices and customer behavior in New York.
Identify factors contributing to higher ratings.
Replicate successful practices in other cities.

**Expected Benefit**: Improved service quality and customer satisfaction across the platform.

### RECOMMENDATION 4: Encourage High-Performing Vehicle Standards
Ford vehicles and the F-150 model received the highest average ratings.

**Recommended Actions**:
Analyze features associated with highly rated vehicles.
Encourage drivers to maintain vehicle cleanliness and comfort.
Provide vehicle quality guidelines and best practices.

**Expected Benefit**: Better ride experiences and higher customer ratings.

### RECOMMENDATION 5: Increase Active Driver Participation
Out of 400 registered drivers, 49 are currently inactive.

**Recommended Actions**:
Identify reasons for driver inactivity.
Offer reactivation incentives and bonus programs.
Improve driver engagement through rewards and recognition programs.

**Expected Benefit**: Increased driver availability and improved service coverage.

### RECOMMENDATION 6: Expand Digital Payment Features
Card payments are the most preferred payment method.

**Recommended Actions**:
Continue investing in secure digital payment systems.
Introduce additional digital payment options.
Provide cashback and reward programs for digital payments.

**Expected Benefit**: Faster transactions, improved user convenience, and increased customer satisfaction.

### RECOMMENDATION 7: Improve Customer Experience Ratings
The average customer rating is 3.67/5, indicating room for improvement.

**Recommended Actions**:
Provide customer service training for drivers.
Monitor low-rated trips and identify recurring issues.
Collect detailed feedback from dissatisfied customers.
Reward consistently high-performing drivers.

**Expected Benefit**: Increased customer satisfaction and higher platform ratings.

### RECOMMENDATION 8: Utilize Customer Feedback More Effectively
More than 9,000 reviews contain written comments.

**Recommended Actions**:
Perform text analysis on customer comments.
Identify common complaints and praise points.
Use feedback insights to improve services and policies.

**Expected Benefit**: Better understanding of customer needs and continuous service improvement.

### RECOMMENDATION 9: Reduce Rider-Initiated Cancellations
Riders account for more than twice the number of cancellations compared to drivers.

**Recommended Actions**:
Improve estimated arrival time accuracy.
Provide clearer driver tracking and notifications.
Introduce small penalties for repeated unnecessary cancellations.
Improve booking confirmation processes.

**Expected Benefit**: Lower cancellation rates and improved operational efficiency.

### RECOMMENDATION 10: Minimize Wait-Time Related Cancellations
Long wait times are among the leading cancellation reasons.

**Recommended Actions**:
Improve ride-dispatch algorithms.
Position drivers strategically in high-demand areas.
Provide real-time wait-time estimates before booking confirmation.

**Expected Benefit**: Reduced cancellations and improved rider satisfaction.

### RECOMMENDATION 11: Prepare for Emergency-Related Cancellations
Personal emergencies are the most common cancellation reason.

**Recommended Actions**:
Maintain flexible cancellation policies.
Distinguish unavoidable cancellations from preventable ones.
Focus operational improvements on controllable cancellation causes.

**Expected Benefit**: Fairer cancellation management and improved user trust.

### RECOMMENDATION 12: Leverage Residential Areas for Growth
Residential zones contain the highest number of locations.

**Recommended Actions**:
Increase driver availability near residential areas during peak commute hours.
Launch targeted promotions in residential neighborhoods.
Analyze pickup and drop-off demand patterns in these zones.

**Expected Benefit**: Higher ride utilization and improved service accessibility.
